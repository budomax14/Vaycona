Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can choose "Continue without an account" to start designing right away. To save, export, print or share your designs, sign in with an account.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email address and tap "Forgot password?". We'll email you a link to reset it. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do I export or print my design?

Open your design and tap the orange Export button at the top of the editor. Choose a format such as PNG, JPG or PDF, or choose Print to print every page of your project.

Where are my projects saved?

When you're signed in, your projects are saved to your account, so you can open them on any device where you sign in to Vaycona.

How do I delete my account?

Open the account menu in the app and choose "Delete account". This permanently deletes your account and your saved projects and uploads. If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

The AI illustration didn't generate.

Check your internet connection and try a different description. Some requests are blocked by our content safety filter. If it keeps failing, contact us.
